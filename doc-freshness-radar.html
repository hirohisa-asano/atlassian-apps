Doc Freshness Radar — Documentation

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Doc Freshness Radar finds stale, outdated and abandoned pages in a Confluence space. It scores every page for staleness and uses Atlassian-hosted AI to tell you which pages to archive, rewrite, or keep.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Open any Confluence space and select Doc Freshness Radar in the space sidebar (under the space's apps).
  3. Select Scan this space. The scan reads every page in the space, scores it, and sends the riskiest pages to AI for review.
  4. Review the table. Each flagged page shows its age, risk score, AI verdict and the reason behind it. Select a page title to open it in Confluence.

Results are saved per space. The next person to open the app sees the latest report without rescanning. Select Rescan this space to refresh.

How the risk score works

Every page is scored from 0 to 100 using these signals:

SignalWhat it detects
Time since last updatePages untouched for 3 months, 6 months, a year, or several years
Draft markersTODO, TBD, FIXME, WIP, DRAFT, and Japanese equivalents (下書き, 作成中, 要更新 and similar)
Outdated year referencesPages whose newest mentioned year is two or more years in the past
StubsPages with very little content
Empty pagesPages with no body content at all

Pages scoring above zero are flagged. The highest-scoring pages are then analyzed by AI.

AI verdicts

VerdictMeaning
staleClearly outdated or abandoned. Archive it or rewrite it.
reviewProbably needs a human to check before deciding.
freshStill accurate. Leave it alone.

Each verdict comes with a one-sentence reason drawn from the page's actual content. AI output is advisory: you decide what to do with each page.

Permissions and data handling

  • Read pages and spaces — needed to scan page content and metadata. The app never edits or deletes pages.
  • App storage — stores scan results (page titles, scores, verdicts) inside Atlassian's Forge storage, within your site's data boundary. Page bodies are not stored.
  • AI — runs on Atlassian-hosted models (Forge LLMs). Content is never sent to any third-party AI provider or to any system outside Atlassian. The app has no external network egress.

See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Limits

  • A scan covers up to 1,000 pages per space. Larger spaces are scanned partially; support for bigger spaces is on the roadmap.
  • AI review covers the 10 highest-risk pages per scan. Remaining flagged pages still show their score and signals.

Troubleshooting

The scan reports fewer pages than the space contains

The app scans pages the app has permission to read, up to the 1,000-page limit. Restricted pages are not included.

No AI verdicts appear

AI verdicts are only generated for pages with a risk score above zero. If nothing is flagged, no AI review runs.

The app does not appear in the space sidebar

Confirm the app is installed for Confluence, then reload the space. Space administrators can manage apps from the space settings.
