Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy applies to the Atlassian Marketplace apps provided by HIROHISA ASANO ("we", "us"), including Doc Freshness Radar, Config Audit Digest and Handover Assistant (each, "the App"). All our apps are built on Atlassian Forge and run entirely inside Atlassian's infrastructure.

What the Apps access

Each App reads only the data required for its feature set, using read-only Atlassian-granted permissions: Confluence page content and metadata (Doc Freshness Radar), Jira audit log records (Config Audit Digest), and Jira issue data (Handover Assistant).

What the Apps store

The Apps store their results (scores, summaries, verdicts and related metadata) in Forge hosted storage provided by Atlassian, inside your Atlassian site's data boundary. Original page bodies and issue contents are not duplicated into storage.

AI processing

Content excerpts are analyzed by large language models hosted by Atlassian (Forge LLMs). Data is processed within the Atlassian platform and is never sent to any third-party AI provider or any system outside Atlassian. The Apps have no external network egress.

What we (the vendor) can access

Nothing. We operate no servers and no databases, and we have no access to your Atlassian content, results, or personal data.

Personal data

The Apps do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to us, and do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking.

Data retention and deletion

Results are retained in Forge storage until overwritten by a newer run or until you uninstall the App, at which point Atlassian deletes app storage per the Forge platform lifecycle.
